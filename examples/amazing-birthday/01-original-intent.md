# 01 — Original Intent

This file summarizes the product intent that emerged at the start of Amazing Birthday development. It is **not a substitute for the verbatim source conversation**. Exact wording belongs in `02-development-transcript/`.

## Initial product idea

Given a person's exact birthdate, produce something substantially more engaging than a conventional "on this day" lookup.

The application should help the reader feel what kind of world they entered, surface a small number of unusually meaningful historical or cultural connections, and connect those moments to the span of the person's lifetime.

## Development stance

No conventional software specification was required before use. The behavior was shaped interactively:

- request an initial result;
- inspect what was useful or unhelpful;
- correct the behavior in natural language;
- reuse the application on another date;
- preserve the accepted behavior once it became valuable.

The transcript is the authoritative record for what was actually said and in what order.

## Why this matters to DbI

The key research question is not whether an AI can generate a birthday report once. It is whether a user can establish a repeatable application behavior through conversation, preserve it, reconstruct it outside the original context, and continue using and modifying it by intent.
