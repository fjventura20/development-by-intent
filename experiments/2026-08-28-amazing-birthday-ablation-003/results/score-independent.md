# BP-AB-ABLATION-003 — ChatGPT Behavioral Scoring

## Evaluator method

I scored the 45 complete trigger outputs in the blinded packet before opening the unblinding key. Each of the seven frozen criteria was scored:

- **0** — absent or materially failed
- **1** — partially present, inconsistent, or substantially weakened
- **2** — substantially present across the condition

The unit of judgment was the condition’s 15 trigger outputs, not the output length. Reconstruction readiness and execution integrity were not used as substitutes for behavioral scoring.

## Evidence basis

### cond-01

The outputs are primarily age, elapsed-time, zodiac, birth-symbol, annual-history, or milestone reports. They do not reliably provide curated exact-date historical connections, significance explanations, lifetime framing, or a concluding synthesis. Some variants add lists of historical events or notable births, but listing is not the frozen behavior.

### cond-02

This group is the strongest of the three, but remains incomplete. Several outputs provide useful historical context and some exact-date events; some entries explain why an event matters. However, the outputs are mostly structured fact lists, vary sharply by session, omit a sustained lifetime arc, and do not end with the required synthesis of the world entered and its transformation. The shorter variants are especially weak.

### cond-03

The outputs consistently provide metadata and a small number of “On This Day” events or fun facts. They generally omit the durable package’s selective narrative judgment, significance explanations, repeated lifetime connections, and closing synthesis. The artifact-only package therefore does not visibly transmit the baseline behavior in this test.

## Blinded conclusion

No blinded condition substantially satisfies the seven-criterion frozen behavioral baseline. cond-02 is the best-performing group, but it is only a partial implementation and does not meet the identity criterion.

The complete evidence supports a negative result for the research question in this bounded experiment: the artifact-only durability package did not transmit recognizable Amazing Birthday behavior more successfully than the alternatives. This does not prove that durability packages cannot transmit behavior; it shows that this package, in this Claude Sonnet 4.6 reconstruction setup and under these conditions, did not preserve the required behavior sufficiently.
